I use AI here and there while making videos and writing this blog. Sometimes it helps me tidy up something I've written, organize my notes, or come up with titles and descriptions.

Occasionally, I might include an AI-made image or a few frames to explain something. When I do, I'll point it out so you can tell them apart from my own photos and footage.

The plant stories and experiences I share are mine. AI helps me put them together, and I decide what makes it into the final video or article.
