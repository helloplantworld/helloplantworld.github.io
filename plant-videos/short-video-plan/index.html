A flexible bank of standalone videos and posts. Choose what you want to film, share it on its own, and connect related stories through a playlist.

Start small and see what connects

Try a small batch across different interests: one watering decision, one cutting, one mix ratio, one LECA check and one progress update. Compare useful audience questions, engagement and interest in follow-ups before filming more. Record setup and progress while caring for your plants.

These ideas are options, not a publishing commitment. Target 60–120 seconds for one clear idea. Keep a video shorter when it is complete, or make a longer companion guide when the demonstration benefits from more time.

A flexible filming structure

  • Open with the question or visible result.
  • Name the plant and relevant growing conditions.
  • Show one action or observation in clear close-up.
  • Explain the decision and what you will check next.
  • Link related published posts and the shared playlist when available.

Processes that take days or weeks become separate dated updates. Each update includes enough context to stand on its own. Numbers below are planning references, not a required episode order.
