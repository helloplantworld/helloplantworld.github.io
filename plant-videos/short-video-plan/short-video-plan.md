# One plant idea in 1–2 minutes

A separate idea bank for standalone videos and posts. These are options to choose from, not a publishing commitment or required viewing order. The [broader original video notes](/plant-videos/video-plan/) remain available separately.

## Make the filming investment count

Start with a small batch across different interests: a watering decision, a cutting, a mix ratio, a LECA check and a progress update. Compare audience questions, engagement and interest in follow-ups before producing more. Record useful setup and progress footage while caring for your plants anyway.

Use 60–120 seconds as a target for one clear idea. Keep a video shorter when it is complete, or make a longer companion guide when context or a full demonstration needs more time. Avoid rushing a procedure to fit the target. Changes that take days or weeks belong in separately dated updates.

## A flexible filming structure

- Open with the question or visible result.
- Name the plant and the conditions that matter.
- Show one action or observation with clear close-ups.
- Explain your decision and what to check next.
- Link a related published post and the shared playlist when available.

Each idea below has its own title and talking points. The source link leads back to its broader preparation notes. Numbers are stable planning references, not episode numbers to put in titles.
