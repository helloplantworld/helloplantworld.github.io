**Course overview · 73 planned lessons · Beginner to advanced**

Understand your plants, make better care decisions, and grow a collection that fits your home. Start with everyday care, then explore propagation, grow lights, LECA, and a small closed terrarium as your confidence grows.

Learn with **Naren, the creator of [Hello Plant World](https://www.youtube.com/@helloplantworld)**, through practical demonstrations using his own plants and growing setups. The course focuses on Indian homes, with Bangalore examples and principles you can adapt to other tropical conditions.

[What you’ll learn](#what-youll-learn) · [Explore the modules](#your-learning-path) · [Choose your practicals](#learn-with-what-you-have)

## Is this course for you?

You might be buying your first plant, trying to understand why your plants struggle, or looking for a more manageable way to care for a larger collection. The lessons increase in difficulty so you can build a foundation before choosing more advanced methods.

You’ll work with familiar plant groups: **pothos, alocasias, philodendrons, monsteras, and anthuriums**. You’ll learn how to adapt care to the plant and your conditions, rather than depend on one fixed watering schedule or potting recipe.

## What you’ll learn

- Choose plants and growing positions that suit your space.
- Assess light, humidity, watering needs, and root health.
- Make your own substrates and measure ingredient ratios confidently.
- Repot, feed, and maintain your plants with a clear reason for each decision.
- Propagate through cuttings, division, corms, offsets, and air layering.
- Investigate problems and follow a plant’s recovery.
- Organize care, space, and records as your collection grows.
- Explore grow lights, IKEA cabinets, LECA, and terrariums when they suit your interests.

## Your learning path

### 1. Start growing indoors

Meet your instructor, get familiar with useful tools, choose a plant for your space, inspect plants before buying, and settle a new arrival into your home. **5 lessons.**

### 2. Understand your growing conditions

Assess window and balcony positions, use a humidity and temperature meter, and adjust care as light, temperature, and drying rates change. **4 lessons.**

### 3. Choose pots and mix your own substrates

Compare cocopeat, coco chips, orchid bark, perlite, pumice, charcoal, and compost. Use a consistent measuring tool to mix by volume and adapt a recipe to your plant, pot, and environment. **7 lessons.**

### 4. Make watering and feeding decisions

Check whether a plant needs water, use a moisture meter alongside other observations, manage drainage, and measure fertilizer dilution. Learn how to choose a feeding frequency for your setup. **6 lessons.**

### 5. Repot and maintain your plants

Recognize when repotting is useful, inspect roots, handle a repot, and observe recovery. Practise leaf cleaning, pruning, and supporting climbing plants. **5 lessons.**

### 6. Get to know your plant groups

Explore dedicated care lessons for pothos, philodendrons, monsteras, alocasias, and anthuriums. See how placement, substrate, watering, and growth habits connect in real plants. **5 lessons.**

### 7. Grow more plants through propagation

Start with identifying nodes and rooting pothos cuttings. Progress to philodendron and monstera cuttings, division, alocasia corms, separating anthurium babies, and air layering. Learn to assess progress and troubleshoot failures, with an introduction to what tissue culture involves. **11 lessons.**

### 8. Investigate plant problems

Build an inspection method for yellow leaves, brown edges, root rot, pests, suspected nutrient deficiencies, and leaf damage. Learn to consider several possible causes, choose a response, and check the result. **7 lessons.**

### 9. Explore grow lights and shelves

Choose and position a light, set a timer, arrange a practical growing shelf, and observe how your plants respond. **4 lessons.**

### 10. Explore an IKEA grow-light cabinet

See how a cabinet can be planned, equipped, arranged, and maintained. Understand the roles of light, airflow, humidity, and access before deciding whether a cabinet suits you. **4 lessons.**

### 11. Try LECA and a low-maintenance money plant

Compare a container without drainage with an inner pot inside a reservoir. Explore plant transitions, nutrient dilution, top-ups, and maintenance. See Naren’s money plant with observed gaps of one to two months between top-ups, and learn what to check in your own setup rather than copying that interval automatically. **6 lessons.**

### 12. Care for a growing collection

Group plants by care needs, organize inspection and watering rounds, keep useful records, and manage incoming plants. Plan for limited space, a realistic workload, and time away from home. **5 lessons.**

### 13. Create a small closed fittonia terrarium

Choose a decorative container, prepare your tools, and follow the planting process. Learn to observe moisture, manage growth, trim your fittonias, and troubleshoot the enclosure. **4 lessons.**

## Learn with what you have

Every practical activity is **optional**. You can follow along, observe a demonstration, or return to a project later. You do not need to build a cabinet, buy a grow light, or try every growing method to use the course.

Begin with your plants and a suitable growing space. Tools are introduced with the tasks that use them: a measuring cup and mixing tub for substrates; scissors, a knife, bottles, containers, and a mat for propagation. Specialized equipment belongs to the optional projects where it is useful.

The course is designed as a library of short videos. Follow the sequence if you’re starting out, or revisit a topic when your plants give you a reason to learn it. Propagation and recovery demonstrations include observations over time so you can understand the process beyond the first step.

## Find your starting point

- **New to indoor plants?** Begin with modules 1–6 to understand your space and everyday care.
- **Ready to propagate?** Build on those skills with modules 7–8.
- **Exploring a new setup?** Choose grow lights, cabinets, LECA, or the terrarium project according to your interests.
- **Your collection is expanding?** Use module 12 to make its care more manageable.
