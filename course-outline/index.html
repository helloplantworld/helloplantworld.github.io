PRACTICAL INDOOR GROWING · COURSE OUTLINE

Grow Indoors with
Hello Plant World

Build confidence from your first plant to a thriving collection. Learn through the creator’s own plants, practical decisions and observed results.

73 short videos13 modulesBeginner → advancedOptional practicals

Designed primarily for Indian homes, with Bangalore examples and principles adaptable to other tropical regions. Featured plants: pothos, alocasias, philodendrons, monsteras and anthuriums, using the varieties already in the creator’s collection.

How to teach and film this course

  • Target 3–7 minutes for most lessons. Give complete demonstrations the time they need. This is a learning sequence, not a release schedule.
  • Show the question, demonstrate your method, explain the decision and include follow-up footage where useful.
  • Every activity is optional. Learners choose projects for their plants, space and budget; no cabinet or equipment build is compulsory.
  • Introduce the workspace tools early and show the relevant equipment before each practical.
  • Film a self-contained segment for Shorts or Reels, with suitable framing and enough context to preserve the advice.
  • Use your actual substrate recipes and feeding routines. Fill in measured ratios, products and plant varieties during filming.
  • Adapt care to changing conditions. Discuss seasons only where a particular plant requires that detail.
  • Use your own practical LECA routine. pH and EC meters, pon, and hands-on tissue culture are outside this course’s scope.

Meet your instructor and welcome to the course

Suggested length2–3 minutes. Film this before the practical lessons.

  • Introduce yourself by name and as the creator of Hello Plant World.
  • Share how you started growing plants and one real success or mistake that shaped your approach; use your own story rather than a scripted biography.
  • Explain why you created this course and how it brings together your practical learnings.

  • Describe the journey from first-plant care to managing a larger collection and mastering propagation.
  • Explain the India-first context, Bangalore growing examples and how learners in other tropical places can adapt the principles.
  • Explain how to use the short lesson library: follow the progression or revisit a specific task; all practicals and equipment builds are optional.
  • Invite learners to start with their existing plants, space and tools, and observe results instead of expecting identical outcomes.

Suggested spoken opening

Hi, I’m [your name], the creator of Hello Plant World. In this course, I’ll share what I’ve learned from growing and propagating my own plants—the methods that work for me, the mistakes I’ve made, and how I make care decisions. We’ll start with the basics and gradually explore propagation, larger collections, LECA, grow lights and more. I’ll use Indian growing conditions, including Bangalore examples, and explain how you can adapt the ideas to your own space. You can follow along with the practicals you enjoy, using the plants and tools you already have. Let’s start by getting to know your growing space.
